    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    How much does a house cleaning cost?
    It depends on your home's size, the type of clean and any extras. A standard clean of a 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom home is about $180. Use the price estimator for your home. Website prices are estimates, not set prices, and are subject to change. We confirm your exact price after seeing photos or taking a quick look at your home in person.
    Do you both come to every clean?
    Yes. Melissa and her son Justin clean every home together, every time. The people you text are the people who show up.
    How does the $25 deposit work?
    Once we've confirmed your price and date, you send a $25 deposit on Venmo to @MSCleaningCo to hold your spot. It counts toward your final price, and you pay the rest after the clean.
    What if I need to cancel?
    Cancel at least 24 hours before your clean and we refund your deposit. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, and no-shows, keep the deposit.
    Do you bring cleaning supplies?
    Yes. We bring the supplies.
    Do you offer discounts for recurring cleans?
    Yes, on Standard and Airbnb cleans: 15% off weekly, 10% off every 2 weeks and 5% off monthly.
    Do you clean homes with pets?
    Yes. Homes with pets usually take a bit longer because of pet hair, so the estimate adds 10%. Let us know about your pets in the quote form.
    How do I pay?
    We take all forms of payment. Venmo is easiest for us: @MSCleaningCo. That covers the $25 deposit to book and the balance after the clean. If you'd rather pay another way, just let us know.
    What areas do you serve?
    Lewisville, DeSoto and across the Dallas–Fort Worth area, with no travel fee. Not sure if you're in our area? Text us your neighborhood at (469) 559-2298.

    Ready for a cleaner home?

    Get your free quote in a couple of minutes. We'll text you back with your price and an open time.
